Ø 3.7 m
  1. Tap View in your space and point the phone at the ground, best outdoors: a square, a park, a street. The sphere appears in front of you at its real size, Ø 3.7 m, screen towards you.
  2. Tap the sphere to see its size. The − and + buttons in front of it switch between the sizes of our flying spheres: 3.7, 4.2, 4.5, 4.7, 5 and 5.2 m.
  3. Drag the sphere with one finger to move it, turn it with two.

On Android phones, pinch with two fingers to change the size instead of the buttons; tap the ground to put the sphere there.

A 3.7 m sphere is taller than most rooms. The phone needs to see the ground where the sphere stands, so very distant spots may not work.
