  1. Choose a size and a height and tap View in your space.
  2. Point the camera at the ground where the sphere should stand: the edge of a square, between trees or houses, above a bus stop. Drag the sphere there with one finger.
  3. Walk around it. The sphere stays where you put it, at the real size shown in the corner, so it looks right next to people, trees and houses.
  4. On Android, change the size with − and + in the corner while you look. On iPhone and iPad, choose the size here before you start.

Sizes follow our size table, from Ø 1.5 to 5.2 m; larger spheres are made to order. The phone needs to see the ground where the sphere stands, so very distant spots may not work. Heights and the tether are only for this preview.
