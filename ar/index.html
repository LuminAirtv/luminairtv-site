Ø 3.7 m
  1. Tap View in your space and point the phone at the ground: a square, a park, a street. The sphere appears there at its real size, Ø 3.7 m, screen towards you.
  2. Tap the ground to put the sphere there, or drag it with one finger.
  3. Pinch with two fingers to change the size. It steps through the sizes of our flying spheres, 3.7, 4.2, 4.5, 4.7, 5 and 5.2 m, and the size in metres appears on the sphere while you touch it.

On iPhone and iPad the camera view is Apple's own: drag the sphere with one finger and resize it with two, but the size in metres is not shown there. For reference, 114% of the start is Ø 4.2 m, 122% is 4.5 m, 127% is 4.7 m, 135% is 5 m and 141% is 5.2 m.

The phone needs to see the ground where the sphere stands, so very distant spots may not work.
