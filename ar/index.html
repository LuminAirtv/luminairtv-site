Loading the 3D sphere…
  1. Choose a height and tap View in your space.
  2. Point the camera at the ground where the sphere should stand: the edge of a square, between trees or houses, above a bus stop. Drag the sphere there with one finger.
  3. Walk around it. The sphere stays where you put it, at its real size of 3.7 m across, so it looks right next to people, trees and houses.
  4. Pinch with two fingers to make it larger or smaller, for example to try a bigger sphere made to order.

The phone needs to see the ground where the sphere stands, so very distant spots may not work. Heights and the tether are only for this preview.
